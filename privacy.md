# Privacy policy: Business Days & Holidays for Jira

_Last updated: {{ site.updated }}._

**Who we are.** Business Days & Holidays for Jira ("the app") is made by {{ site.vendor }}, {{ site.location }}. Contact: [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).

**What the app stores.** Only the calendar settings that your Jira administrators enter: calendar names, country and region, weekend days, time zone, cutoff time, closure dates and names, extra working days, excluded holiday names, and the Jira project keys mapped to each calendar. The app stores no personal data about your users.

**What the app reads but does not store.** When someone opens the issue panel, the app reads that work item's due date, created date and resolution date, as that user, to work out business days. These values are used for that one calculation and then discarded. Automation actions receive only the inputs your rule passes them.

**Where data lives.** Everything stays on Atlassian's infrastructure (Forge hosted storage and compute). The app is eligible for Atlassian's "Runs on Atlassian" program: it sends no data outside Atlassian and supports Atlassian data residency.

**Sharing.** We don't sell or share data, and we don't use third-party analytics.

**Retention and deletion.** Calendar settings are kept while the app is installed. When you uninstall the app, Atlassian deletes its Forge storage under Atlassian's data-retention policy. You can delete individual calendars from the admin page at any time.

**Logs.** If a calculation fails, the app may write an error message to Atlassian's Forge logs. These logs contain no user identifiers or work-item content and are kept by Atlassian under its retention policy.

**Your rights.** Because the app stores no personal data, there's usually nothing to access or delete about an individual. Contact us with any privacy question at [{{ site.support_email }}](mailto:{{ site.support_email }}).

**Changes.** We'll post updates here and change the date above.
